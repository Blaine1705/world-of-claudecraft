# Drowned Temple Warden character asset

Original Blender-authored Drowned Temple guardian. The user-supplied
`proposal.png` establishes the ancient stone guardian, bronze trident, coral
and restrained cyan accents. The geometry is original, deliberately translated
into chunky KayKit proportions; no source image pixels, downloaded mesh,
third-party character or textures are embedded.

The final Warden replaces the rejected box-built guardian entirely. Its primary
volumes are hand-profiled elliptical mesh lofts: a continuous tapered cuirass,
domed shell pauldrons with rolled edges, shaped cuisses, greaves and vambraces.
The closed ceremonial visor has narrow cyan slots and a central shrine crest,
without exposed lips, nose or horns. Curled fingers grip a slender trident with
swept outer tines. Small coral branches, three barnacles, two short kelp ribbons
and shallow plate seams suggest long submersion without noisy surface detail.

The shipped Sanctum Boneguard and Knight Commander Olen informed the curved
armour silhouette, with the original temple guard providing marine ornament.
These are visual references only; no mesh or texture was copied. The silhouette
stays stylized and clean, rather than anatomical realism. Stable source and
runtime keys retain `tidemarked_sentinel` despite the display-name change.

The source of truth is
`scripts/assets/drowned_temple_creatures/tidemarked_sentinel.py`.
The adjacent `.blend` is an editable Blender 5.2.1 delivery generated by the
same source. It uses the existing temple `build_creature.py`, `sea_kit.py`
and Hollow Crypt rigid skin/export helpers. Shared source changes require
rebuilding and checking the resulting GLB.

## Build

Run from the repository root, substituting the installed Blender executable:

```powershell
blender -b --factory-startup --python scripts/assets/drowned_temple_creatures/build_creature.py -- tidemarked_sentinel tmp/asset_src/temple_tidemarked_sentinel.glb --blend scripts/assets/drowned_temple_creatures/tidemarked_sentinel.blend
node scripts/assets/build_assets.mjs scripts/assets/specs/temple_tidemarked_sentinel.json
node scripts/assets/compress_glb_textures.mjs public/models/creatures/temple_tidemarked_sentinel.glb
node scripts/build_media_manifest.mjs generate
```

Create `tmp/asset_src` before the export if missing. The optimization item is
`character`, so it never simplifies skin weights. Texture compression is a
verified no-op because all surfaces use vertex colors. Geometry is meshopt.

## Contract and budget

- Shipping path: `public/models/creatures/temple_tidemarked_sentinel.glb`.
- Blender: yards, +Z up, facing -Y. GLB: +Y up, facing +Z, no yaw correction.
- Native skinned bounds: sole Y 0.04000, Head-weighted crown Y 3.62962 and
  trident tip Y 4.21000. The actual body height is 3.58962 yards; the complete
  mesh height is 4.17000. Runtime `height` normalizes those complete bounds.
- Manifest height 4.027 times the unchanged mob template scale 1.5 produces
  a body height of approximately 5.2 yards, twice the 2.6-yard player body.
  The acceptance test includes both multipliers and excludes the weapon tip.
- Thirteen rigid skin joints, two material primitives, 11,242 triangles.
  `Weapon` is a stable joint parented to `Fore.R`; all trident vertices bind
  to it, while the hands remain part of the arm. No extra material draw is added.
- Budget declared before authoring: 12,000 triangles, 20 bones, four primitives,
  500 KiB, zero textures. Delivery is approximately 131 KiB.
- Clips: `Idle`, `Walk`, `Run`, `Attack`, `Attack2`, `Hit`, `Death`, `Cast`,
  authored at 24 fps. The standard `TEMPLE_CLIPS` map is sufficient.
- Death tips backwards and ends in a held grounded pose. Every joint has
  translation, rotation and scale tracks, preventing cold corpses inheriting
  either the bind pose or a previous attack. Upper arms remain beside the body.
- Small eyes, chest gem and trident seal use explicitly cyan emission. Vertex
  colors do not tint glTF emission, so white emissiveFactor is deliberately avoided.
- Asset-local `SentinelBody.finish` preserves the already-linear palette through
  Blender's BYTE_COLOR transfer. Without this correction Blender linearizes the
  values a second time, crushing stone and bronze in the actual temple lighting.
  Body surfaces remain normally lit and non-emissive; palette values are tested.

`tests/tidemarked_sentinel_asset.test.mjs` parses the shipped compressed GLB,
pins source/output hashes, checks the rendering/animation budgets and measures
the final corpse skeleton rather than merely checking that a Death clip exists.
Its body-height test decodes quantized positions through joint world matrices
and inverse bind matrices, then checks Head-weighted crown vertices against the
soles so the trident cannot artificially inflate the claimed body size.

The production GLB was inspected through the shipped browser loader from front,
rear, side and in its final Death pose. This checks compression, actual material
appearance and corpse silhouette independently of the Blender scene.
